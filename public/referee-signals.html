Boarding R41
Clenched fist strikes the open palm of the other hand, in front of the chest.
Charging R42
Clenched fists rotate around each other in front of the chest.
Checking from behind R43
Both arms push forward from the chest, palms open and facing away, at shoulder level.
⚠ Open palms. Cross-checking uses fists.
Cross-checking R59
Both fists clenched, pushed forward and back from the chest about 30 cm.
⚠ Fists. Checking from behind uses open palms.
Tripping · Kneeing · Clipping R57 · 50 · 44
Hand strikes the leg, skates on the ice. ① Below the knee = Tripping ② Knee = Kneeing ③ Behind the knee = Clipping.
Elbowing R45
Tapping either elbow with the opposite hand.
Check to head or neck R48
Open palm of the non-whistle hand pats the side of the head.
Roughing · Fighting R51
Clenched fist, arm extended straight out to the side.
⚠ Fist. Offside called is an open hand.
Holding · Holding the stick R54
Clasp one wrist in front of the chest. Holding the stick: then both hands as if on a stick.
Hooking ↔ Spearing R55 · 62
Both arms tug toward the stomach = Hooking. Jab out, then drop the hands = Spearing.
Interference · Icing called R56 · 81
Arms crossed, still, in an X in front of the chest. Referee = Interference · linesperson after the whistle = Icing.
Butt-ending R58
Closed-fist forearm moves under the other forearm, held palm down.
High-sticking R60 · 80
Both fists clenched, one above the other at forehead height, as if holding a stick. Same signal for high-sticking the puck.
Slashing R61
Chopping motion with the edge of one hand across the opposite forearm.
Delay of game R63
Flat hand slides up from the other (lower) hand at the centre of the body.
Unsportsmanlike · Diving R75 · 64
Both hands on hips, plus two fingers as appropriate.
Too many players R74
Six fingers (one open hand + one finger) in front of the chest.
Penalty shot R24
Both arms crossed over the head.
Goal R78
Outstretched hand points at the goal the puck legally entered.
Wash-out R—
Sweep both arms out to the sides, palms down, at shoulder level: no goal, no offside, no icing, etc.
Delayed offside · Possible icing R83 · 81
Non-whistle arm straight up, flat hand. Lowered to the side = cancelled.
Offside called R83
Whistle, then arm extended horizontally, pointing at the blue line.
⚠ Open hand. Roughing is a fist.
Hand pass R79
Open palm facing forward, pushed forward once or twice.
Time-out R87
Both hands form a T in front of the chest.
Face-off violation warning R76
One arm bent, open palm up, on the side of the team that committed the violation.
